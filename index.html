How it works

From closet to finished look in four steps.

  1. Add your dresses

    Scan with your camera or pick photos from your gallery. Save them to My Closet.

  2. Describe the event

    Choose the theme, vibe and city, and add a few words about the occasion.

  3. Get your best match

    Kimikoo picks the dress that fits the event and explains why in a few lines.

  4. Finish the look

    Add bags, shoes, accessories, hair and perfume from your closet or new picks.

Start in about 5 minutes

How to join the beta

  1. Open Kimikoo on your phone.
    kimikoo.vercel.app
  2. Add it to your home screen.
    1. Open the link in Safari
    2. Tap Share (square with an up arrow)
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add
    1. Open the link in Chrome
    2. Tap ⋮ (three dots, top right)
    3. Tap Install app, then Install
  3. Sign up with your email. You'll get a code. If it doesn't arrive in a minute, check your spam folder.
  4. Add at least 5 items and style one real event you have coming up.
  5. Tap Send feedback in your Profile, or reply to us on WhatsApp.
